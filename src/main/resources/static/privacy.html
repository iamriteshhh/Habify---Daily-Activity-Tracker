🔒 Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 16, 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

At Habify ("we", "our", or "the platform"), your privacy, personal discipline, and autonomy are our highest priorities. Habify was built with a fundamental architectural commitment: zero telemetry, zero invasive tracking, and maximum user control.

This Privacy Policy explains how Habify collects, uses, hashes, stores, and protects your information across our web application and API services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly required to deliver habit tracking, analytics, and account synchronization:

A. Account & Profile Information

  • Full Name: Used for personalized display across your workspace.
  • Email Address: Used for account identification, verification, and password recovery.
  • Username: A unique, lowercased identifier for authentication.
  • Password Hash: We never store your plaintext password. All passwords are processed with salted BCrypt cryptographic hashing.

B. Activity Tracking & Performance Data

  • Habits & Routines: Habit titles, categories, priorities, schedules, target units, and time-of-day preferences.
  • Daily Activity Logs: Completion status (COMPLETED, PARTIAL, MISSED), completion percentages, numerical progress, timestamps, and optional user notes.
  • Milestone Goals: Target goals, target values, units, deadlines, and progress states.

C. Guest Mode (Local Storage Only)

If you choose to use Habify without registering for an account, your habits and daily tracking logs remain exclusively on your personal device in your browser's localStorage. Our servers receive and store zero data from guest sessions.

3. Cryptographic Security & Protection Standards

We implement defense-in-depth engineering to ensure that your sensitive credentials and recovery tokens cannot be harvested, even in the event of an unauthorized database compromise:

  • Password Hashing: Passwords are protected using multi-round BCrypt with per-user salt. Plaintext passwords never touch our database or log files.
  • Token Hashing (SHA-256): Single-use password reset tokens and email verification tokens are hashed using HMAC SHA-256 before persistence. If a database dump were leaked, an attacker cannot reverse or use the tokens.
  • Session Revocation & Versioning: Every user account maintains a cryptographic token_version. When you change your password or click Logout, all active JSON Web Tokens (JWTs) across all devices and browsers are immediately revoked on the server.
  • Anti-Spoofing Rate Limiting: Authentication endpoints are governed by a thread-safe sliding window rate limiter (15 requests/minute) anchored strictly to remote peer IP addresses to neutralize brute-force attacks and IP spoofing.
  • Formula Injection Neutralization: Exported CSV reports automatically sanitize leading special characters (=, +, -, @) to prevent DDE / spreadsheet execution vulnerabilities.

4. What We Never Do

Our business model is centered on productivity software, not advertising or user surveillance. We explicitly promise that:

  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data or habit history to any third party or data broker.
  • We do not use third-party tracking scripts (such as Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar, or advertising tracking beacons).
  • We never access your camera, microphone, device location, or biometric sensors.
  • We do not store payment or financial card details on our servers.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

You maintain full ownership of your data at all times:

  • Data Portability & Export: You can export your full habit matrix and activity logs into structured CSV format at any time via the Reports interface.
  • Account Deletion: You have the right to request the complete and permanent deletion of your account and all associated habits, logs, and goals. Once deleted, data cannot be recovered.
  • Token Expiration: Password reset links automatically expire in 15 minutes. Email verification links expire in 48 hours. Expired tokens are immediately cleared.
