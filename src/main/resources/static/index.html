Core Features

Designed for Long-Term Consistency

Habify turns raw habit tracking into actionable clarity, motivation, and discipline analytics.

📅

Smart Habit Scheduling

Define habits that match your real lifestyle. Set routines to run daily or on designated days of the week (e.g. Mon, Wed, Fri).

🔥

Rest-Day Aware Streaks

Rest days shouldn't break your momentum. Habify's frequency-aware streak engine ensures non-scheduled days protect your hard-earned streak.

📊

Rolling Discipline Score

Measure your real consistency with a 7-day rolling score ($0-100$). Partial efforts are rewarded, and missed scheduled days offer constructive feedback.

📈

Day-by-Day Visualizer

Inspect your weekly consistency at a glance with clean day-by-day bar charts showing completion percentages and scheduled routines.

⚡

1-Click Fast Tracking

Zero friction daily logging. Mark habits as Complete (100%), Partial (with a custom % and note), or Missed with a single touch.

🎯

Clear Routine Management

Organize habits by categories (Study, Coding, Fitness, Reading, Health, Finance). Pause or resume habits anytime without losing historical logs.
